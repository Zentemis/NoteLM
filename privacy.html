Your Audio Never Leaves Your Device

NoteLM is built with a privacy-first architecture. Here's what that means for you:

🖥️

100% Local Generation

All text-to-speech runs in your browser using Kokoro ONNX models via WebGPU or WASM. No audio data is ever sent to a server — we physically cannot access your recordings.

🚫

Zero Data Collection

No analytics, no tracking cookies, no user accounts. We don't know who you are and we don't want to.

📦

Static Site Only

NoteLM is a purely client-side app hosted on GitHub Pages. There is no backend server — nothing to breach, nothing to leak.

📥

Your Audio, Your Control

Generated audio exists only in your browser's memory. When you download it, it's a local file transfer — not a server download.

Our Commitment

NoteLM was designed from the ground up to respect your privacy. We believe powerful AI tools don't need to come at the cost of your data. Every voice, every word, every generation — it all stays with you.
